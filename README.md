### Hi, I'm Citina 👋

I'm a PhD candidate in Industrial & Systems Engineering at USC Viterbi. I build simulation models of how people behave and how diseases spread, so a public health strategy can be tested on a model before anyone uses it for real. Along the way I use data analytics, machine learning and causal inference.

Published work (code pinned below):
- **Mpox in Los Angeles County:** [Nature Communications (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71993-w), co-first author; [Open Forum Infectious Diseases (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1093/ofid/ofae401), first author
- **COVID-19 and HIV in San Francisco:** [JAIDS (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1097/QAI.0000000000003156), first author

When daily life annoys me, I build the small tool that fixes it and keep it running. I build them with Claude Code. Most are about parking:
- [USC Ticket Clock](https://citina.github.io/la-streets/): when enforcement actually comes by each street around campus
- [LA Street Rules](https://citina.github.io/la-streets/streets/): street sweeping, meter patrols and other ticketed rules on any block in Los Angeles
- [SF Streets](https://citina.github.io/sf-streets/): whether you can park on a San Francisco block, and what it's like on foot
- [LA Walking](https://citina.github.io/la-walking/): police reports and pedestrian crashes around any spot in Los Angeles
- [Curb Log](https://citina.github.io/curb-log/): how much street parking is free near USC, by half hour
- [Credit Card Benefit Tracker](https://github.com/citina/credit-card-benefit-tracker): daily reminders for use-it-or-lose-it card credits, inside my own Google account
